Personal Music Library Transfer — Terms of Use

Effective October 24, 2026. Version 2026-10-24.

Personal Music Library Transfer is Brian Borg’s private tool for transferring selected music-library references into his own Google account. It is not a public service. Using the client means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Use requires authorization from the account owner and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, Google’s applicable terms and the tool’s Privacy Policy.

The tool creates private playlists, inserts approved recording references and follows approved artist channels as instructed. It does not transfer audio ownership or guarantee that a recording will remain available. Matching may be incomplete; uncertain or unavailable recordings remain for review. API quotas and Google service availability can delay completion.

The owner may stop the tool and revoke its Google authorization through Google Account permissions. Stopping or deleting local records does not remove changes already saved in YouTube. Where applicable deletion deadlines permit, preserve progress records until any unresolved writes have been reconciled to avoid duplicate transfers. Deletion requests and mandatory deletion deadlines take precedence; do not restart a transfer after deleting its records without reconciling existing destinations.
