Personal Music Library Transfer

A private command-line tool operated by Brian Borg to transfer his selected iTunes/Apple Music playlist references into his own YouTube account.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. It preserves source order and repeated songs, creates private playlists, follows verified artist channels, and checks saved results before resuming. It does not transfer audio files. Uncertain or unavailable matches remain for manual review.

Private access

This page explains the tool. It does not offer public sign-in, a hosted migration service or access to Brian’s music library. Google reviewers receive separate demonstration materials. There are no charges, advertising or public data feeds in this tool.

Your controls

The local client requires agreement to its Privacy Policy and Terms of Use before API access. It provides a data inventory and a preview of local deletion or token revocation. Existing content in YouTube is preserved.

You can revoke Google access at Google Account permissions. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact briborg21@gmail.com.

Google’s Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service apply to Google services.
